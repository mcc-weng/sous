G對話 — three answers to the chat problem

GYou've put your finger on a real conflict. A book is finished; a conversation is happening. Chat bubbles on cookbook paper look like a messaging app wearing a costume — and a third theme would leave the app speaking three visual languages, which is one more than a premium product can hold together.

My read: the problem isn't the theme, it's the metaphor. "Personal chef in your pocket" isn't a chef you text — it's a chef who leaves you things: a note on the counter, a correction in the margin, a plan on the table. So the three options below aren't three skins for a chat screen; they're three answers to what a conversation with him even is. G1 and G2 stay on the settled stock (F1 red on F4 structure); G3 deliberately breaks it — a third room is exactly the trade-off it exists to test.
