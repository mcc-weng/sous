SOUS · M3小當家 — 書與灶 · the settled system

The book you own, and the stove you stand at. Everything is a page in a finely printed cookbook — #EDEAE2 stock, #22201C ink, one seal red #9B2C1E per page, centred labels tracked wide, generous margins, hairline rules, Noto Serif TC for names and verdicts. The only dark screens are the ones where something is running and your hands are busy: the stove steps, the timers, 上菜. Crossing happens once per session and both directions are designed — 開始做菜 dims the page, 上菜 prints the night back into it.

Talking to him is not a chat app. You ask from the page you're on and his answer is written into that page (邊欄); anything that belongs to no page lands in a single 便條 thread. He is never "online" — he wrote this book, and he answers.

I動態 — 過場 · 發牌 · 揭曉(press 播放)

Two curves: settle cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1) for anything that lands, heat cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1) for anything that glows or fills. Three buckets: 140ms feedback, 280ms transition, 620ms ceremony — except the crossing, which is deliberately the longest thing in the app. Reduce Motion collapses everything to a 140ms cross-fade; the reveal keeps its cover, because the drama is the two-step, not the movement.

What never animates: ingredient and shopping checkmarks (instant — the stamp is the receipt), shopping rows re-sorting (they'd move under a wet finger), step text in Cook Mode (140ms opacity swap, no slide — the eye must not chase it at the stove), and page folios. The burner flare from v1 is gone from paper entirely — heat now only exists in 灶, which is exactly the point of the split.

Paper helps more than it costs: ink on cream at 11px already clears AA where brass on black at 9px did not, and daylight legibility in a supermarket is solved by the ground itself. Three things still need work — gesture-free paths, CJK at AX sizes, and the fact that a blurred cover means nothing to VoiceOver.
