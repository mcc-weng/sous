SOUS · M3小當家 — full screen set · 菜牌 skin

The system. Black stage · warm white ink · one heat accent #FF8A28 that appears only where something is live: heat, the day being decided, a verdict. Noto Serif TC carries dish names, verdicts and counts — the formal voice. Noto Sans TC carries UI and body copy, mostly at weight 300 so the serif reads as the voice of authority. Cards are objects: #141516, 1px warm hairline, 22px radius, light pooled beneath the plate. No second accent, no shadow except under a card, no emoji outside the persona mark. Every value here is persona-tintable — swapping --tint and the two warm tones is the whole persona swap.

Both ritual models are real screens below: the conversational Q&A that ships today (section B) and the swipe session (section C). A light theme of the same system is at the end.

H明亮模式 — the same system in light

Dark stays the default. Light is a token swap, not a redesign: same layout, same serif voice, same one accent. Three changes make it work — the ground becomes warm paper (#F7F5F1) and cards become true white with an ink hairline; the accent darkens to #C25A0E so it still passes contrast on paper; and the glow stops being a bloom and becomes a soft warm wash under the plate (a light bloom on a light ground just reads as dirt). Heat is then signalled by the accent and by weight, not by luminance.

L動態 — motion spec (press each to play)

Two easing curves only. settle cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1) for anything that lands (cards, sheets, rows) and heat cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1) for anything that glows or fills. Durations come from three buckets: 140ms feedback, 280ms transition, 620ms ceremony. Nothing in the app animates longer than 900ms except the standing pulse on the presence dot. All of it respects Reduce Motion by collapsing to a 140ms cross-fade — the reveal keeps its cover so the drama survives without movement.

throw 280ms settle — the card leaves along the drag vector, rotation continues at the same rate it had at release (never a fixed angle), opacity to 0 at 70% of travel. The next card runs settle 280ms from scale .95/y+11 with a 40ms delay, so the stack visibly steps forward rather than teleporting. Stamps do not animate; their opacity is bound to drag distance and simply freezes at release.

One shimmer for every awaiting-brain state: drag-to-swap, a note being answered, a chat-driven plan edit. 1.2s loop, accent at 16% — visible but not a progress bar, because the duration is unknown. It replaces the row's own content only when the row has nothing truthful to show; otherwise it washes over the old value, which stays readable and tappable.

What never animates: ingredient checkmarks (instant — the tick is the receipt), shopping rows re-sorting (they'd move under a wet finger), step text in cook mode (it swaps at 140ms opacity, no slide, because the eye must not chase it at the stove), and the presence dot's pulse never stops or speeds up as a status signal.

M無障礙 — non-gesture paths, large type, VoiceOver

Three risks this design carries and how each is closed. Gesture-only decisions — every swipe already has a button, but the buttons must be labelled with the outcome, not the gesture. CJK at large Dynamic Type — the tight tracked labels and 5-across trays break first; they reflow rather than truncate. The reveal — a blurred cover is meaningless to VoiceOver, so it gets an explicit two-step announcement. Targets are ≥44pt everywhere; the accent is never the only carrier of meaning (每個狀態都有文字).

Also required, no mockup needed: Reduce Motion collapses every animation in section L to a 140ms cross-fade (the reveal keeps its cover — the drama is the two-step, not the movement); Reduce Transparency swaps every glow for a flat card fill; Increase Contrast lifts hairlines from 12% to 30% and inkFaint to inkDim; the deck supports VoiceOver's escape gesture to leave a ritual without swiping; and all timers fire a haptic and a sound, since the phone is usually across the counter.
